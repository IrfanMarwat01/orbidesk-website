Privacy Policy

How OrbiDesk AI collects, uses, protects, and shares information across our AI WhatsApp receptionist platform.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

OrbiDesk AI ("OrbiDesk AI", "we", "us", or "our") provides an AI-powered WhatsApp receptionist and clinic-automation platform for healthcare clinics. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when clinics ("Clinics") use our platform and when patients interact with a Clinic through it. OrbiDesk AI is operated by Irfan Ullah Khan as a business registered in Pakistan, and operates under Pakistani law.

1. Our role: processor for Clinics

OrbiDesk AI is a business-to-business platform. When it comes to patient information, the Clinic is the data controller and OrbiDesk AI acts as a data processor that handles patient information on the Clinic's behalf and instructions. Each Clinic is responsible for obtaining any consents required from its patients. For information about a specific Clinic's own privacy practices, please contact that Clinic.

2. Information we collect

Clinic and account information

When a Clinic signs up and onboards, we collect information such as the Clinic's name, services, doctors, working hours, contact details, and the WhatsApp Business credentials needed to connect the service.

Patient conversation information

To provide the service, we process the messages patients send to the Clinic's WhatsApp number, along with related details such as name, contact number, appointment requests, and preferences that patients share in conversation.

Usage information

We collect technical and usage information (such as log data and analytics about how the platform is used) to operate, secure, and improve the service.

3. How we use information

  • To operate the AI receptionist: answering patients, booking and rescheduling appointments, sending reminders, following up, and recovering missed appointments.
  • To provide each Clinic with its dashboard, analytics, and configuration tools.
  • To provide customer support and onboarding.
  • To secure, maintain, and improve the platform.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

OrbiDesk AI does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or make medical decisions. All medical decisions remain with the Clinic's clinicians.

4. WhatsApp and service providers (sub-processors)

The platform operates through the official Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, and patient messages are transmitted through WhatsApp in accordance with Meta's terms and policies. To run the service we use the following providers, each of which processes information only to deliver its service to us:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform): delivers messages between patients and the Clinic.
  • Google (Google Cloud and the Gemini AI service): application hosting in Mumbai, India, and generation of AI replies to patient messages.
  • Supabase: database and login accounts, hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • Redis Cloud: message-processing queues, hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • Sentry: error monitoring, hosted in the United States.

Because some of these providers operate outside Pakistan, information may be processed in other countries, including India and the United States.

5. How we store and protect information

Clinic and patient information is stored on access-controlled cloud infrastructure, and sensitive credentials, such as a Clinic's WhatsApp access token, are stored encrypted. Each Clinic's data is kept separate: every dashboard request is tied to the signed-in user's Clinic, so one Clinic cannot see another Clinic's patients, conversations, appointments, or staff. In addition, row-level security is enabled on every database table, and only OrbiDesk's own servers can change stored records. Access is role-based and authentication is required for dashboard requests. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we apply safeguards appropriate for healthcare clinics.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with: (a) the Clinic to whom it belongs; (b) service providers acting on our behalf under confidentiality obligations (such as the hosting, database, AI, messaging and monitoring providers listed in section 4); and (c) authorities where required by applicable law.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service to the Clinic. Clinic admins can delete individual patient records from the dashboard at any time. When a Clinic's account is deleted, we remove the Clinic's records, its staff login accounts and its uploaded photos, and we remove our subscription to the Clinic's WhatsApp Business Account. Copies held in our providers' backups may remain until they expire under those providers' normal schedules, and some information may be kept where the law requires it.

Data deletion requests. Full instructions are on our data deletion page. To ask for information about you to be deleted, email help@orbideskai.com with the phone number you used on WhatsApp and the name of the clinic you contacted. We pass the request to the Clinic, which controls patient records, and help the Clinic carry it out.

8. Your choices and rights

Patients who wish to access, correct, or delete their information should contact the Clinic they interacted with, as the Clinic controls that data. Clinics can manage and delete their data through the dashboard or by contacting us. Where applicable law grants additional rights, we will honor them in accordance with that law.

9. Children's privacy

The platform is intended for use by Clinics and their staff, not directed at children. Any information about minors that a patient or Clinic shares is handled by the Clinic as controller and should follow the Clinic's own policies and applicable law.

10. Governing law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of Pakistan. We handle information in line with applicable Pakistani law.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify Clinics.

12. Contact us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to help@orbideskai.com, or sent on WhatsApp through our contact page. OrbiDesk AI is operated by Irfan Ullah Khan, Pakistan.
