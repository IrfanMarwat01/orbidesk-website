● The 24/7 loop

Five steps that never sleep

Every patient conversation flows through the same reliable loop — no missed messages, no forgotten follow-ups.

💬1. Message

Patient texts your WhatsApp.

🤖2. Reply

AI answers in seconds.

📅3. Book

Appointment confirmed.

🔔4. Remind

Reminders sent automatically.

💸5. Recover

No-shows & leads re-engaged.

1

The patient messages your clinic

A patient sends a WhatsApp message to your clinic's own number — a new enquiry, a booking request, or a question about services. No app to download, no form to fill. Just the channel your patients already use every day.

Assalam o Alaikum, do you treat acne scars?
And what are your timings tomorrow?
Wa Alaikum Assalam! Yes, we offer acne-scar treatments. Tomorrow we're open 11 AM–8 PM. Would you like me to book you a consultation? 😊
2

The AI replies within seconds — in their language

OrbiDesk understands the question and responds in seconds, matching the patient's language and switching automatically if they change it mid-chat. It answers from your clinic's own knowledge base, so replies are accurate and on-brand — never a missed message, even at midnight.

3

It books the appointment for you

Once the patient is ready, the AI checks availability, offers real slots, and confirms the booking — capturing the patient's details in the process. Rescheduling and cancellations are handled the same effortless way.

📅
Appointment bookedConsultation · Tomorrow, 4:30 PM
🔔
Reminder sent"See you tomorrow at 4:30 PM"
4

It reminds patients so they show up

OrbiDesk automatically sends reminders before each appointment to prevent no-shows and the revenue loss they cause. Patients can confirm, reschedule, or cancel right in the chat.

5

It recovers the revenue you'd otherwise lose

Missed appointments and unbooked enquiries don't just disappear. OrbiDesk follows up on missed visits, re-contacts patients who asked but didn't book, and reminds past patients when their next check-up is due.
