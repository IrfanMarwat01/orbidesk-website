Data Deletion

How to ask for your information to be deleted from OrbiDesk AI.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This page explains how to ask OrbiDesk AI to delete information about you. It applies to patients who have messaged a clinic on WhatsApp, to clinic staff, and to clinics that use OrbiDesk AI. For how we handle information in general, see our Privacy Policy.

If you are a patient

A clinic that uses OrbiDesk AI controls the records of its own patients. To ask for your information to be deleted:

  • Email help@orbideskai.com.
  • Include the phone number you used on WhatsApp and the name of the clinic you contacted.
  • Say what you would like deleted, for example your whole chat history and patient record.

We pass your request to the clinic, which controls patient records, and help the clinic carry it out. You can also ask the clinic directly.

If you are a clinic

  • Clinic admins can delete individual patient records from the dashboard at any time.
  • To delete your clinic's whole account, email help@orbideskai.com from the address registered to the clinic. When a clinic account is deleted, we remove the clinic's records, its staff login accounts and its uploaded photos, and we remove our subscription to the clinic's WhatsApp Business Account.

What to expect

Copies held in our service providers' backups may remain until they expire under those providers' normal schedules, and some information may be kept where the law requires it. Questions about a request can be sent to help@orbideskai.com.
