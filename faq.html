● FAQ

Everything you need to know about OrbiDesk AI

Clear answers about features, setup, security, and how OrbiDesk AI runs your clinic on WhatsApp — automatically.

About OrbiDesk AI

What is OrbiDesk AI?
OrbiDesk AI is a WhatsApp AI receptionist built specifically for clinics. It handles patient conversations, books appointments, sends reminders and follows up on missed visits and enquiries, automatically, 24/7, directly inside WhatsApp. No new app for patients to download, and no extra reception staff to hire.
Who is OrbiDesk AI built for?
OrbiDesk AI is built for specialized clinics — dental clinics, dermatology clinics, aesthetic/cosmetic centers, plastic surgery clinics, and physiotherapy centers — from single-doctor practices to clinics with several doctors.
Is OrbiDesk AI a medical or diagnostic tool?
No. OrbiDesk AI is strictly a clinic communication and automation platform. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe medication, or replace medical advice from a qualified clinician. All medical decisions remain with the clinic's doctors.
Which country and laws does OrbiDesk AI operate under?
OrbiDesk AI is a Pakistan-based platform and operates under Pakistani law. Billing is processed in PKR (Pakistani Rupees) and contracts are governed by Pakistan's commercial regulations.

Features & Capabilities

What can the AI receptionist actually do on WhatsApp?
It replies to patient enquiries, books, reschedules and cancels appointments against real availability, sends reminders and preparation notes, checks in after treatment, follows up on missed visits and unbooked enquiries, reminds patients when a check-up is due, and alerts your team when your escalation rules are met.
Does OrbiDesk AI support multiple languages?
Yes. OrbiDesk AI replies in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, in the same language the patient uses. It also understands voice notes.
How fast does the AI respond to patients?
Replies usually arrive in well under half a minute, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights, weekends and holidays.
Can the AI book appointments by itself?
Yes. OrbiDesk AI handles end-to-end booking: it checks doctor availability, matches the requested procedure to the correct duration, blocks the time slot, confirms with the patient, and writes the appointment to the clinic's dashboard automatically.
Can OrbiDesk AI recover missed appointments and no-shows?
Yes. When a visit is marked as missed, OrbiDesk sends the patient a friendly message offering a new time. It also reminds past patients when their next check-up is due.

Setup, Onboarding & WhatsApp

How long does it take to get OrbiDesk AI live?
Setup is guided and needs no technical knowledge. You add your clinic details, doctors and hours, and we help you connect your WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official platform.
Do I need a WhatsApp Business account?
Yes. OrbiDesk AI connects through the official Meta WhatsApp Business Platform, and we guide you through connecting your number during onboarding.
Will my existing WhatsApp number change?
In most cases you can keep your clinic's existing number, so patients keep messaging the number they already know. We check your number's setup with you during onboarding.
Do my patients need to install any new app?
No. Patients simply message your clinic on WhatsApp the way they already do. OrbiDesk AI works invisibly behind that same number.

How much does OrbiDesk AI cost?
Pricing is tailored to your clinic's size and needs. Book a free demo and we'll walk you through a plan and quote that fits your clinic. Billing is in PKR (Pakistani Rupees).

Data Security & Privacy

Is my clinic and patient data secure with OrbiDesk AI?
Yes. Every clinic operates inside its own isolated tenant. Strict row-level security ensures no clinic can ever see, query, or access another clinic's patients, conversations, appointments, or staff. Authentication is enforced on every dashboard request.
Where is my data stored?
All clinic and patient data is stored on encrypted, access-controlled cloud infrastructure with role-based access. Only authorized users from your clinic (and you, the clinic admin) can read or modify your data.
Can I delete my patient data?
Yes. Clinic admins can delete a patient's records from the dashboard. When a clinic account is closed, all of that clinic's data is deleted.

Comparison & Alternatives

How is OrbiDesk AI different from a regular WhatsApp chatbot?
Generic chatbots follow rigid scripts. OrbiDesk AI is an autonomous AI receptionist built for clinics: it knows your treatments and doctors, books against real availability, remembers patient history, and alerts your team when your rules say a person should step in.
How is OrbiDesk AI different from hiring a human receptionist?
A human receptionist works fixed hours, handles one chat at a time, takes leave, and costs a recurring salary. OrbiDesk AI works 24/7, handles many conversations at the same time, remembers each patient, and works alongside your team.
Why not just use ChatGPT or a generic AI tool?
Generic AI tools have no knowledge of your clinic's doctors, procedures, prices or schedules. They cannot book real appointments, cannot manage no-shows, and cannot integrate with WhatsApp Business. OrbiDesk AI is a turnkey clinic system, not a generic chat box.

Support & Help

How do I get support if I have a problem?
You can reach the OrbiDesk AI team by email or WhatsApp from the Contact page. We help every clinic through onboarding.
Do you provide training for clinic staff?
Yes. Every onboarding includes a guided walkthrough of the dashboard and the AI settings.
What happens if the AI cannot answer a patient's question?
When a conversation meets the escalation rules you set in the dashboard, OrbiDesk AI alerts your chosen staff on WhatsApp with a summary so a person can step in.
