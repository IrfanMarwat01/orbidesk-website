● FAQ

Questions clinics ask us

Does it use my clinic's own WhatsApp number?

Yes. OrbiDesk connects to your clinic's WhatsApp Business number so patients talk to "you," with the AI handling replies within seconds.

Will it sound robotic to my patients?

No. The AI is tuned for clinic conversations and your knowledge base, so it replies naturally and on-brand — in your patients' language.

Is my patient data safe?

Each clinic's data is kept separate, and staff see only their own clinic. We walk you through our privacy approach during your demo.

How long does setup take?

Setup is guided and needs no technical knowledge. You add your clinic details, doctors and hours, and we help you connect your WhatsApp Business number through Meta's official platform.
