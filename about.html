● About us

Built to amplify clinic teams,
not replace them

We started OrbiDesk AI after watching clinic receptionists struggle with dozens of WhatsApp messages while managing a full waiting room. We knew AI could help — not replace staff, but amplify them.

● Our values

What we build around

🎯
Mission-Driven

OrbiDesk AI helps clinics automate patient communication and daily operations — from automated booking to smart follow-ups — reducing missed appointments and keeping schedules full, without extra staff.

🛡️
Secure by Design

Patient data is handled with a privacy-first approach: secure channels, controlled access permissions, and strict data isolation so only authorized clinic staff can view sensitive information.

🌐
Multilingual by Design

OrbiDesk AI understands real clinic workflows and how patients actually interact. Whether they text in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, the AI replies in the same language.

👥
Clinic-Centric

Every feature is built around real clinic needs — booking, reminders, follow-ups, and workflow integration. OrbiDesk AI fits how your clinic already works, not the other way around.

🕐
Automation That Saves Time

Reduce staff workload with automated scheduling, timely reminders, and no-show recovery — clinic automation that handles repetitive tasks so your team can focus on patient care.

💬
WhatsApp-First Communication

Patients interact via WhatsApp — no app to install. A familiar, effortless experience for patients while automating your clinic's WhatsApp communication 24/7.
