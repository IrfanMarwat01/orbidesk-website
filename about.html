● Our values

● Founder

Meet the founder

Dr. Irfan Ullah Khan

Founder & CEO, OrbiDesk AI
  • MBBS doctor
  • Built OrbiDesk AI end to end
  • Based in Pakistan

Dr. Irfan Ullah Khan is a medical doctor (MBBS) and the founder of OrbiDesk AI.

Medicine showed him how much of a clinic's day goes into WhatsApp: patients asking about prices, timings and appointments, often after hours, while the front desk is busy with the people already in the room. Messages wait, and a patient who doesn't get a reply often books somewhere else.

He built OrbiDesk AI to close that gap, and took it himself from the first idea to a live product: the AI receptionist that answers patients on WhatsApp in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, the booking, reminder and follow-up system behind it, and the dashboard clinics use every day. He built it with modern AI development tools, so every workflow in OrbiDesk was shaped by someone who understands both the clinic and the software.

His goal is simple: no patient message left unanswered, and more of the clinic's time for care.
